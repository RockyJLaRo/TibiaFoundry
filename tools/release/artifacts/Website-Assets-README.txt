tibiafoundry.com - website assets for Tibia Foundry {VERSION}
=============================================================

TibiaFoundry-{VERSION}-Website-Assets.zip holds every image and icon of the built website ({COUNT} files),
each at the path the pages load it from. Copying the contents of TibiaFoundry-{VERSION}-Website-Assets/ over
the root of the deployed site puts every asset where the pages expect it.

Structure (inside TibiaFoundry-{VERSION}-Website-Assets/)
  img/                       shared images: the logo, the pixel-art samples made by the Content Studio and the
                             English screenshots (light and dark), as WebP; pixel art and logo are lossless
  img/pt-br/ img/pl/ img/es/ img/de/
                             the same screenshots with the program in that language
  favicon.ico                16, 32 and 48 px
  favicon-192.png            192 px icon
  apple-touch-icon.png       180 px icon for home screens
  og-image.png               1200x630 link preview (English)
  og-image-pt-br.png, og-image-pl.png, og-image-es.png, og-image-de.png
                             link previews of the other languages

Where they come from
  All of them are generated by website/build_site.py from website/src/images (see the website source package):
  screenshots are converted to WebP, the icons and link previews are drawn from the logo. Nothing here is
  edited by hand; to change an asset, change its source image and build the site again.

The HTML pages, CNAME, robots.txt and sitemap.xml are not in this package: they are in website/site of
TibiaFoundry-{VERSION}-Website-Source.zip.
