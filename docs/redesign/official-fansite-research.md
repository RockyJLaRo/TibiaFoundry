## How each site was checked

| Method | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **Headless capture** | `research/tools/capture_fansites.py` (Playwright, Chromium 140 user agent): home page at 1440×900 and at 390×844 (phone, touch, 2× DPR), up to three internal pages chosen from the site's own navigation (tools, calculators, guides and statistics first), viewport and full-page screenshots, and a JSON record of title, language, navigation, footer text, search fields, fonts, colours, horizontal overflow, consent banners and ad markers. |
| **Visual review** | Every desktop and phone screenshot was looked at (contact sheets `research/sheets/desk-1…6.jpg`, `phone-1…4.jpg`). |
| **In-app browser** | Sites that refused the headless browser were opened in the desktop app's own browser; text and structure were read from the live page. Screenshots from that browser are not saved as files. |
| **Secondary sources** | Only where a site could not be opened at all: the official directory entry and indexed search results. Marked as such. |

Human-verification (CAPTCHA) pages were **not** bypassed.

### Inaccessible or partially accessible
- **PortalTibia.com.br** and **Tibia-Statistic.com**: Cloudflare human-verification page in both the headless and the in-app browser; not bypassed. Only the directory entry and search results were used (search results mention long-form interviews with CipSoft staff on PortalTibia).
- **TibiaWiki (Fandom)**: blocked for the headless desktop browser, available on the headless phone browser and in the in-app browser.
- **TibiaWiki.com.br** and **TibiaVip.app**: home pages accessible, internal pages blocked (403) for the headless browser.
- **TibioPedia.pl**: a splash page ("Wejdź / Enter") in front of all content; the capture recorded the splash.

## Cross-site synthesis

**What makes a site feel like Tibia while staying modern.** The convincing examples (TibiaDaily, TibiaMisterios, TibiaRoute, TibiaDraptor) keep the game's character in a few places – a masthead, frames, gold accents, game or pixel icons – and keep the reading surface plain and high-contrast. Sites that put texture or painted art behind text (TibiaLight, Rookie, TibioPedia) lose readability and usually also phone support.

**Dense data.** The best data pages (TibiCam, TibiaRoute, GuildStats) put a search or filter first and show results as plain tables; the worst wrap data in decoration or push it below a hero.

**Navigation that scales.** Icon + label lists (TibiaDraptor sidebar, TibiaMisterios tool grid, TibiaRoute icon rail) scale to dozens of tools; flat uppercase bars with 40–80 links (TibiaDuality, TibiaDecorations, TibiaLife) do not.

**Daily facts are the common return hook.** Boosted creature/boss, Rashid's location, server save and events appear on BomDiaTibia, TibiaRoute, TibiaVip, TibiaWiki and TibiaDaily – players expect them.

**Mobile.** 6 of the 30 sites that loaded are not usable on a phone (fixed 980–1100 px layouts or horizontal overflow: GuildStats, TibiaGoals, TibiaWiki.com.br, TibioPedia, TibiaEvents, TibiaLight). A fully mobile-first site is still a differentiator.

**Footers that feel complete** (FooTibia, TibiaDaily, Hunt-Analyser, TibiaWars) combine: what the site is, CipSoft attribution and non-affiliation, data credits, privacy/cookie links and contact.

**Recurring weaknesses to avoid.** Uncontrolled ad networks (finance clickbait on TibiaQA, video and retail ads over TibiaWiki.com.br content), splash/gate pages, banners covering content, very long home pages (13,000–42,000 px), menus inside `<select>` elements, low-contrast text on images.

**Monetisation transparency examples.** TibiaHome (donations in Tibia Coins), TibiaVip (VIP for Tibia Coins removes ads), TibiaMaps.io (Donate tab), GuildStats (donors list).

## How the research changed Tibia Foundry

| Finding | Applied in the redesign |
|---|---|
| Search-first headers (TibiCam, GuildStats, TibiaLife) | Character search is the first interactive element of the home page, in the header from 1280 px and at the top of the phone menu |
| Daily facts bring players back (BomDiaTibia, TibiaRoute, TibiaWiki) | "Today in Tibia" panel on the home page: boosted creature, boosted boss, players online – all live, nothing invented |
| Icon tiles for tools (TibiaMisterios, TibiaDraptor) | Original 16×16 pixel icons in stone tiles on every tool card, the hub and the phone menu |
| Medieval but readable (TibiaDaily) vs texture behind text (TibiaLight) | Stone and battlements only on the header/footer frame; parchment panels carry plain high-contrast text (WCAG AA checked) |
| Many sites fail on phones | Mobile-first layouts, checked at 320–1920 px with zero page overflow |
| Long home pages | Home trimmed to six focused sections; the phone page shrank after removing duplicate feature cards |
| Complete footers (FooTibia, TibiaDaily, TibiaWars) | Footer with purpose, tagline, creator and email, six profiles, grouped links, data credits, CipSoft links, Tibia Rules, legal page, dynamic year |
| Analytics consent (TibiaWars) and monetisation statements (TibiaVip, TibiaHome) | Legal page: "Money and advertising" (none) and "Privacy and statistics" with a statistics opt-out |
| Uncontrolled ads (TibiaQA, TibiaWiki.com.br) | No ads; documented as a rule in the compliance checklist |
