# Tibia Foundry – future feature roadmap

Built from `official-fansite-research.md` and `tibia-tools-ecosystem-research.md` (research of 17–18 November 2026).
**Status 2026-11-19: items #1, #2, #3, #6, #7, #9, #10 and #11 shipped in calculators batch 1 (see `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-calculators-batch1.md`); all other items are still not implemented.** "Researched" items cite observed tools or gaps;
"Speculative" items are ideas that still need demand evidence.

Principles for every item: no accounts, no personal data, nothing scraped beyond TibiaData's public API, formulas shown with
their source and the Tibia version/date they were checked against, user-editable prices, results that work on a 360 px phone,
five languages from day one, graceful behaviour when TibiaData is down (static calculators keep working; data views show the
last good answer marked as stale – already implemented).

## Executive shortlists

**Top 10 most valuable additions** (value × frequency ÷ maintenance): 1 Calculator framework (#1) – everything else depends on it; 2 Formula registry (#2) – the trust layer competitors lack; 3 Party loot splitter (#5) – used after every team hunt; 4 Exercise weapons with editable prices (#4) – fixes a measured 25 % disagreement; 5 Experience & level (#3); 6 "Today in Tibia" extended (#7); 7 Stamina (#6); 8 Imbuement cost (#8); 9 Boss kill statistics view (#14); 10 Character progress since last visit (#12).

**Top 5 recurring-usage drivers:** #7 daily panel (every day), #5 loot splitter (every party hunt), #6 stamina (daily), #14 boss kill statistics (daily for boss hunters), #12 character progress (each session).

**Top 5 quick wins** (low complexity): #3 experience & level, #6 stamina, #11 share range (inside #3), #9 blessings, #10 character stats – all stable official formulas, no external data, once #1/#2 exist.

**Competitor-weakness opportunities:** hidden price assumptions in exercise calculators (#4); planners that overflow phones (Wheel of Destiny 914 px, imbuement 414 px → #8, #17); ad-heavy unsourced calculator farms ranking in search (#1–#3 with sources); dead or repurposed tools (tibiabosses.com, TibiaWiki BR experience page) – a maintained set with "verified on" dates.

**Differentiation opportunities:** transparency (formula, source, version, editable assumptions) as a visible standard (#2); fully mobile-first and five-language from day one; no accounts and local-only data (#5, #12); the only site that serves fansite authors (#15 + the desktop app).

## Dependencies and maintenance

- **#1 and #2 first.** Every calculator relies on the shared template, the formula registry and its known-value tests.
- **After each Tibia update:** review `formulas.json` entries tagged with changed systems; update "verified on" dates; re-run known-value tests.
- **TibiaData outages:** calculators (#3–#11, #16–#18) are static and unaffected; data features (#12–#14) show cached answers marked as stale (existing behaviour).
- **Privacy:** nothing leaves the browser except TibiaData requests already disclosed on the Legal page; local storage keys must be listed there when added.
- **Protected paths:** none of these features may touch `games/`, `pokemon/`, `tools/`, `.github/`, `CNAME` or `robots.txt`; a future service worker (#23) must be scoped so it cannot control those paths.
